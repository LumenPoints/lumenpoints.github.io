10. CONTROLS FOR DO-NOT-TRACK FEATURES

Most web browsers and some mobile operating systems and mobile applications include a Do-Not-Track ("DNT") feature or setting you can activate to signal your privacy preference not to have data about your online browsing activities monitored and collected. At this stage, no uniform technology standard for recognizing and implementing DNT signals has been finalized. As such, we do not currently respond to DNT browser signals or any other mechanism that automatically communicates your choice not to be tracked online. If a standard for online tracking is adopted that we must follow in the future, we will inform you about that practice in a revised version of this Privacy Notice.

California law requires us to let you know how we respond to web browser DNT signals. Because there currently is not an industry or legal standard for recognizing or honoring DNT signals, we do not respond to them at this time.

12. AI HEALTH DATA PROCESSING

When you use LumenPoints AI-powered features — including Stack & Blood analysis, Skin & Goals analysis, Skin Routine analysis, Hidden Signals, Body Stack analysis, Trends correlation, and Protocol Recommendations — your health data (including supplement information, bloodwork markers, body metrics, skincare routine details, mind and mood ratings, and any images or documents you upload) is transmitted to the Anthropic Claude API (a third-party AI service) for processing. This data is used solely to generate the educational insights and observations shown to you within the app. Data sent to the Anthropic Claude API is retained by Anthropic in accordance with their standard commercial API data-handling policies, which include brief retention (typically up to 30 days) for trust-and-safety review. LumenPoints does not access this retained data. Anthropic does not use the data to train models. For details, see Anthropic's data usage policy at https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy. LumenPoints does not train, tune, or maintain its own AI models. All AI insights are generated by the Anthropic Claude API and are informational only. They do not constitute medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or a medical opinion, and do not create a doctor-patient or other professional relationship. You should not disregard, avoid, or delay seeking professional medical advice because of any AI-generated insight. LumenPoints does not sell your health data to any third party.
