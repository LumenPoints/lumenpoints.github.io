Privacy Policy

Last updated September 16, 2026 · LumenPoints, LLC

LumenPoints is a wellness tracker. The data you put into it is among the most personal information you have, so the rules are short and we hold to them: never sold, never shared, never used to train AI.

What we collect

Only what the app needs to work.

  • Account information — your email address and an authentication credential, so you can sign in on a new device.
  • What you log — supplements and medications you track, body measurements, bloodwork files you upload and the markers read from them, skincare products and routines, and journal, mood, sleep, and gratitude entries.
  • Health data you connect — if you enable Apple Health, we read only the categories you approve, and only to display and trend them in the app. We do not write to Apple Health unless you ask us to.
  • Basic technical data — crash reports and error logs used to fix defects.

We do not collect advertising identifiers and we do not run third-party advertising or marketing analytics in the app.

How your data is used

  • To show you your own records, trends, and reminders.
  • To generate educational observations across the Points you track.
  • To operate, secure, and support the service, and to respond when you write to us.

That is the whole list. Your data is not used for any purpose you did not ask for.

AI processing

Observations are generated by a large language model operated by Anthropic (Claude), called through our own backend. The relevant portions of what you have logged are sent to that model to produce the observation and the result is returned to you. Under our agreement with Anthropic, your content is not used to train models. Observations are educational and informational only.

Where your data is stored

Your records are stored in our database, hosted in the United States, with encryption in transit and at rest. Access is limited to the small number of people who need it to operate the service and to answer your support requests.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your data. There is no ad-supported revenue path.
  • We do not use your data to train AI models.
  • We do not share your data with third parties for marketing or analytics.
  • We do not disclose your data except to the service providers required to run the app, or where the law requires it.

Your choices

You can edit or remove any individual entry at any time. You can delete your account from inside the app, which deletes your records, your uploaded files, and your account itself. Deleted data is removed from active systems immediately and from routine backups within 30 days. You can also write to support@lumenpoints.com to request a copy of your data or ask us to delete it for you.

Children

LumenPoints is not intended for children. Do not use the app or create an account if you are under 13.

Not a medical service

LumenPoints is a wellness app, not a medical service, and is not a HIPAA-covered entity. AI-generated observations, status indicators, and any insights are educational only and do not constitute medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult your physician before making changes to your supplements, medications, or health routine.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and, for anything material, tell you in the app. We will not make a change that would allow us to sell your data or use it to train AI models.
